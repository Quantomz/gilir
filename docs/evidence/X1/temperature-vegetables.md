## Decision standard

Spec 3.3 requires recorded applicability, temperature variable, averaging period and sensitive stage. Spec 5.3 requires evaluating the exact variable, period and sensitive stage supported by the source. A limitation that admits a conversion does not establish that conversion. Here, `unsupported` means that the complete encoded mapping lacks support in the accessed evidence. It does not mean that temperature has no effect or that every source number is false. Access gaps are identified below and in the JSON.

The 13 records are in [x1-temperature-b.json](x1-temperature-b.json). Three rules also occur in release `fao56-vegetables-2026-11-15`. All 13 occur in `fao56-temperature-rules-2026-11-15`.

## Findings

| Rule | Independently observed evidence | Operational finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `potato-frost-whole-tmin-minus2.5` | [Seppanen 2001, pp.153, 156, Table 1 and pp.157-158, Table 2](https://jukuri.luke.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/4f896264-3ad3-4add-91bd-98e97ae29696/content) distinguishes cellular leaf LT50 and canopy, ground and 2 m air readings. Its abstract says -2.5 to -3.0 C; Table 1 includes Puikula at -2.4 C. | Daily screen-air Tmin <= -2.5 C across the whole occurrence does not preserve the tissue measurement or exposed-foliage context. The asserted warmest endpoint also differs from Table 1. |
| `potato-night-heat-development-tmin-18` | [Gautam 2024, Introduction](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2024.1364244/full) states nights above 18 C reduce tuberization. [Kim and Lee 2019, Methods and Table 2](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2019.00300/full) compares 14 days of canopy-air night means of 17.8 and 21.7 C during initiation, with Superior in Korea. | These support neither daily Tmin >18 C nor a one-day onset. The night mean and FAO development-stage conversions remain assumptions. The original derivation of 18 C was not recovered. |
| `broccoli-frost-tmin-minus7` | [Luo 2011, section 2.2 and Table 2](https://pagesperso.locean-ipsl.upmc.fr/escape/luo2011.climaticchange.pdf) and [Tan original journal summary, DOI 10.1071/EA99037](https://connectsci.au/an/article-abstract/39/6/771/144822/Freeze-induced-reduction-of-broccoli-yield-and?redirectedFrom=fulltext) agree on field lethality between -7 and -9 C. Tan induced extracellular freezing with a -2 C/hour cooling ramp; stage and acclimation affect responses. | The numeric range is supported. Exact support for daily screen-air Tmin <= -7 C over every stage is absent. Full methods, including hold duration and measurement position, were inaccessible. This is an evidence gap within the unsupported mapping, not proof that -7 C weather is harmless. |
| `broccoli-heat-mid-tmax-35` | [Luo, section 3.3, pp.593-594](https://pagesperso.locean-ipsl.upmc.fr/escape/luo2011.climaticchange.pdf) describes Galaxy exposed to 35 C for one week during inflorescence production/floral initiation. Shorter exposure caused little injury. | Daily Tmax >35 C changes the period and stage. Its strict inequality also excludes the studied 35 C treatment. Bjorkman and Pearson 1998 original full text was inaccessible. |
| `cauliflower-curd-development-tmean-20` | [Rosen 2018, Introduction and phenology model, pp.1-4](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2018.00078/pdf) states >20 C can delay induction in the summer-cultivar context. Its own model accumulates genotype-specific development rates from daily mean air temperature after juvenility. | Daily mean is a source variable, but the paper does not establish a binary daily >20 C predicate throughout FAO development. It models cumulative induction. Wiebe 1980, underlying the introductory statement, was not read. |
| `lettuce-bolting-tmax-30` | [Qin 2020, Introduction and Plant materials and treatment](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0244198) cites >30 C background. Its GB-30 experiment used sustained 33/25 C versus 20/13 C from sixth true leaf, sampled through day 32. [Jenni and Yan 2009 abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1439-0523.2009.01657.x) concerns ten crisphead varieties under Quebec field environments. | Whole-season daily Tmax >30 C is not established by either accessed account. Jenni and Yan full text was unavailable. |
| `spinach-germination-initial-tmean-20` | [Leskovar 1999, pp.301-303, Methods/Results, Fig.2](https://journals.ashs.org/downloadpdf/view/journals/jashs/124/3/article-p301.pdf?inlineView=true&pdfJsInlineViewToken=1817361258) incubated seeds for two weeks on moist filter paper in Petri dishes. Inhibition began above 20 C in Cascade/ACX5044 and above 23 C in Fall Green. Alternating regimes changed germination. | Daily screen-air mean >20 C changes the measured environment, period and stage. The experiment measured laboratory seed incubation, not field air or soil. Even a soil conversion needs separate support. The full FAO initial stage also exceeds germination. |
| `sweet-pepper-heat-mid-tmax-32` | [Aloni 2001 primary abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1034/j.1399-3054.2001.1120407.x) compares Mazurka at 32/26 C versus 28/22 C for eight days before anthesis. [Karni and Aloni 2002 primary abstract](https://academic.oup.com/aob/article-abstract/90/5/607/205151) compares 32/26 C versus 26/22 C. | Retaining only 32 C as daily Tmax >=32 drops the night treatment and duration. A two-regime contrast does not locate a daily onset. Both full texts were inaccessible. |
| `sweet-pepper-cold-mid-tmin-12` | [Darnell 2012 primary abstract](https://academic.oup.com/aob/article/110/5/987/171134) compares 12 C and 20 C night treatments crossed with fruiting status. Responses at 18 and 40 days differ; low-night, non-fruiting plants show strongest swelling. | Daily Tmin <=12 C over mid-season removes chronic exposure and source-sink dependence. The contrast does not identify an onset. Full text was inaccessible. |
| `tomato-heat-mid-tmean-29` | [Pressman 2002, Introduction and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4240456/) reports Peet's 29 versus 25 C daily means; its own Trust experiment applies continuous 32/26 C. [Sato 2002 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11971929/) identifies sensitivity 7-15 days before anthesis and relief periods exceeding five days. | Daily mean is accurate, but a single-day >=29 C rule over FAO middle changes the exposure period and sensitive window. Peet 1997 original and Sato 2002 full text were not read. Sato 2006 full text was inaccessible and was not used as independent proof. |
| `tomato-heat-mid-tmax-40` | [Pressman Discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4240456/) paraphrases Iwahori as above 40 C for hours at meiosis. [Iwahori 1965 original abstract and linked PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjshs1925/34/1/34_1_33/_article) specify exactly 40 C for three hours on each of two days in Fukuju No.2. | A daily maximum cannot establish hours at temperature or two treatment days. The >40 predicate excludes the original 40 C treatment. FAO middle does not identify meiosis. |
| `watermelon-heat-mid-tmax-32` | [Tao 2026, section 3.1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2026.1902742/full) is authentic, published 26 August 2026. It reports daytime 32 versus 27 C and female-flower development. Section 6.4 explicitly calls for intensity, duration, timing and genotype to be considered. | Daily Tmax >=32 C and FAO middle are unsupported transformations of that contrast. Rudich and Peles 1976 and Manzano 2014 originals remained inaccessible; no original-method claim rests on search snippets. |
| `green-bean-night-heat-mid-tmin-27` | [Konsens 1991 author-university abstract](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/the-effect-of-temperature-on-the-production-and-abscission-of-flo/) confirms 17 versus 27 C night treatments in determinate Tenderette; anthesis and early pod development were most sensitive. | A controlled night setpoint is not daily Tmin. The accessible abstract does not identify exposure duration or a >=27 C onset. Whole mid-season is broader than the stated physiological window. Full text was inaccessible. |

These results support retaining the underlying literature observations with their limits. They do not support inventing replacement daily thresholds from treatment contrasts. Root X1 adjudication owns the resulting tests and profile-owner follow-up.
