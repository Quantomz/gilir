What we flagged
We flag a crop if, in any past season, even one week had less rain than it needs, or
a temperature outside its limits. The check is strict, so most plans have warnings.
•    Rain fell short of what Rice (paddy) needs in past seasons
     In some replay seasons, rainfall fell below estimated crop demand in at least
     one rolling window.
A warning doesn’t mean the crop fails, and it isn’t a prediction for next season. It
shows where past seasons fell short, so you know what to watch.



Good to know
•    About the growing details for Rice (paddy)
     Paddy rice: the rainfall-demand screen excludes ponding, seepage and
     percolation water requirements, so it understates the water a flooded paddy
     needs.

•   Rainfall alone supplies every candidate. No irrigation supply or farm soil-water
    balance is assumed.
•   Ranking minimizes frequency, relative mismatch, unplanted days, then longest
    unplanted gap. Temperature indicators do not enter that tuple.
•   Comparisons use 6 decimal places. Displayed assessment values remain
    unrounded.
•   Ranking dates use a 7-day grid plus window edges, baseline planting dates,
    and earliest field availability. This does not establish daily optimality.
•   The independent prefix budget is a deterministic safety bound. Representative
    workload benchmarks remain pending.
•   No pest or weed assessment, yield forecast, soil improvement, fertilizer
    quantity, or cost estimate is made.
•   The reported historical duration has no unambiguous released stage allocation.
•   Stage lengths are FAO-56 Table 11 average examples for the stated region
    and planting period, not local observations.
•   FAO-56 Table 11 row used: Rice | 30 | 30 | 60 | 30 | 150 | Dec; May | Tropics;
    Mediterranean (Tropics, December planting).
•   Region rectangle is an approximate bounding box drawn around the place
    named in FAO-56 Table 11; it is not a published boundary.
•   FAO-56 Table 12 values for a sub-humid climate (RHmin about 45%, u2 about
    2 m/s) under standard non-stressed conditions; no climatic adjustment is
    applied.
•   Table 12 'Rice' row; Kc ini 1.05 assumes 0.10-0.20 m standing water in a
    sub-humid climate with light wind (Table 14 gives 1.00-1.20 by climate).
•   Kc end 0.90 is the higher value of the Table 12 range 0.90-0.60, which has no
    footnote; the higher value gives the more cautious water-demand estimate.
•   Gilir planning assumption: 7 days to clear and prepare the field after harvest.
    FAO-56 gives no turnaround; this is not a published agronomic value.
