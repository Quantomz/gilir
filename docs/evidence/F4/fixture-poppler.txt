What we flagged
We flag a crop if, in any past season, even one week had less rain than it needs,
or a temperature outside its limits. The check is strict, so most plans have
warnings.
•     Rain fell short of what synthetic crop needs in past seasons
      Example rainfall warning.
A warning doesn’t mean the crop fails, and it isn’t a prediction for next season. It
shows where past seasons fell short, so you know what to watch.
