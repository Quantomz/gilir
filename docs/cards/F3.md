## Outcome

A farmer can understand the one suggested rotation, its risks and supporting calculations, or the specific reason a plan is unavailable.

Read [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/Quantomz/nefalos_nasa_hackathon/blob/chore/parallel-build-baseline/AGENTS.md), activate pstack, and use the frozen [contracts](https://github.com/Quantomz/nefalos_nasa_hackathon/blob/chore/parallel-build-baseline/docs/contracts.md). ## Implement

- [ ] Render all six PlanResult branches. Only plan and plan_with_warnings show a generated timeline. Show warning text above the timeline, not only in details.
- [ ] Show one view-only suggested plan, the selected priority indicators, actual cohort count/period, search incompleteness, and missing/unassessed checks.
- [ ] Display kept/changed/unassessable/skipped baseline states accurately. Show crop-order/date changes and source-backed preparation from the snapshot. Do not invent a roadmap or improvements.
- [ ] Implement View details with source references, profiles, assumptions, metrics, units, denominators, and method versions.
- [ ] Wire Change inputs to the supplied callback, PDF through exportPdf, and local save/clear through localPlans. A storage/export failure remains visible while the result stays readable.

## Done when

- [ ] Every synthetic PlanResult state is inspected at phone and desktop widths. Missing-data and impossible-calendar fixtures have no fake generated bars.
- [ ] The warning fixture retains risk language and search-limit disclosure. The kept-baseline fixture recommends retaining the sequence.
- [ ] Keyboard users can open details and activate regeneration/export. No direct editing of output bars exists.
